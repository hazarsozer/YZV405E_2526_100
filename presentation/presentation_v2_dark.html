03 · PIVOT What Actually Moved the Score

Captions outperform vision–text alignment.

The original SigLIP2 image–text pipeline scored 0.30. Switching to BGE-M3 text matching against English captions jumped to 0.37 — and nothing later beat it.

  • SigLIP2's text encoder is weak on non-English and idiomatic input, even after NLLB translation
  • Image captions are always in English — a clean, single-language signal channel
  • BGE-M3 is built for multilingual semantic similarity; query and captions live in the same space
  • Adding SigLIP2 image features back on top of BGE captions did not help — text channel dominates

06 · DIAGNOSIS Two Ceilings, Compounding

The 0.37 ceiling has more than one source.

Our submission attributed the ceiling to the visual metaphor gap. A post-hoc audit of NLLB output found a second, upstream cause — translation also destroys idioms before they reach BGE. Concrete: Turkish "çürük elma" becomes "rotten elmas".

WHAT WORKED

  • Classifier-gated query selection — paraphrase for idiomatic, original for literal. +0.07 over the proposed pipeline.
  • BGE-M3 over SigLIP2 for the text channel — English caption alignment beats fragmented multilingual vision-text.
  • 94.1% sense classification from a single calibrated LR head on top of frozen BGE-M3.
  • Compact footprint — 3.5 GB peak VRAM via sequential model loading, no fine-tuning.

THE 0.37 CEILING

  • Translation gap (upstream) — NLLB-200-distilled-600M corrupts idioms during translation for many of the 13 zero-shot languages. Compounds left untranslated, surrounding context hallucinated. Signal is lost before BGE matching even begins.
  • Modality gap (downstream) — captions describe what an image shows; idioms describe what it means. Text-text similarity cannot bridge this.
  • Next steps split cleanly: stronger MT (NLLB-3.3B, LLM-MT) for the upstream gap; VLMs with metaphor priors (GPT-4V, LLaVA-Next) for the downstream one.
